Changes: resized to 256 x 160 and WebP-encoded (quality 82); recon.webp is the frames encoded and decoded by
nvidia/Cosmos-Tokenize1-DV8x16x16-720p. NVIDIA's video guardrail (safety classifier, then face blur) ran on them
and on the generated frames: all frames passed; faces were pixelated in 2 real frames.
